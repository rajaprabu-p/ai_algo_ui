RP Algo

Personal Algorithmic Trading Dashboard

RP Algo is a private web dashboard used by its owner to run and monitor a personal, rules-based trading strategy for NIFTY index options on the National Stock Exchange of India. It is used to:

  • backtest the strategy on historical market data,
  • run paper trading (simulated trades with no real money),
  • review strategy logs, trade history and daily reports.
